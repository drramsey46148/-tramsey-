What this site is about

New Hunters Indiana provides beginner-focused information on deer hunting in Indiana: how seasons work, legal requirements, essential gear, safety practices, and ethical hunting. We emphasize hunter education, respect for landowners, and proper field care.

About deer hunting

White-tailed deer are the most commonly hunted species in Indiana. Hunting strategies depend on season, local habitat, and deer movements. Deer are crepuscular—most active at dawn and dusk—so planning hunts around those times is effective.

Understand local regulations (licenses, bag limits, and seasons) before you plan a hunt.

Public vs Private land

Public land: State forests, wildlife areas, and some federal lands are open to public hunting but may have specific rules—portable stands may be allowed but permanent stands are often prohibited on state land. Always check property rules before leaving stands or blinds. Indiana DNR.

Private land: Always obtain written permission from landowners before hunting. Do not alter land or install permanent structures without permission. Respect property boundaries and local rules.
