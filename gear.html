Gear checklist

  • Indiana hunting license and identification
  • Appropriate firearm or bow (and safety gear)
  • Blaze orange vest/hat (when required)
  • Hunting boots with good traction
  • Warm layers and waterproof outerwear
  • Field dressing kit: sharp knives, gloves, game bags
  • Navigation: maps, GPS, compass
  • Communication: cell phone, whistle
  • Game hoist or straps for transport
  • First aid kit and emergency supplies

Stand & Camera basics

Portable tree stands and ground blinds are commonly used; never leave permanent stands on public/state land and mark stands with owner's info if leaving during allowed periods. Trail cameras are useful for scouting — place them on game trails and check regulations for use on public lands.
