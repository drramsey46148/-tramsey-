Starter Gear Checklist

Below is a simple packing list to get started.

  • Indiana hunting license and ID
  • Appropriate firearm or bow + safe storage
  • Orange clothing (vest/hat)
  • Hunting boots
  • Knife and field-dressing kit
  • GPS or maps and compass
  • First aid kit

Beginner Tips

Safety
Always treat firearms as they are loaded, keep the muzzle pointed in a safe direction, and be sure of your target and what's beyond it.
Scouting
Make sure you scout early for trails, rubs, and signs. Use maps and talk to local land managers for permission and info.
Scent Control
Pay attention to wind and use scent-reducing practices when possible like evercalm, and Dead down Wind products..
