Overview of seasons

Indiana has multiple deer seasons (archery, youth, firearms, muzzleloader, and deer reduction zones). See the Hunting & Trapping guide for county-specific dates and bag limits.

Typical season types (check DNR for current dates & zones)
SeasonTypical RangeNotes
Reduction ZoneSept - Jan (zones vary)Special rules and additional bag limits in designated zones.
YouthWeekend prior to Oct 1 (example)Short weekend for youth hunters.
ArcheryOct - JanOften overlaps other seasons.
